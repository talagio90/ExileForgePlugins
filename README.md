# Exile Forge Plugin

An add-on app for **Path of Exile 1** from the Exile Forge team. It draws a radar of the area you are in, counts kills, marks the beasts and uniques you care about and, if you switch them on, can help with routine tasks.

**It is included with Exile Forge Premium.** The app uses the Premium key you already activated in Exile Forge; without an active Premium key none of the plugins can be switched on.

## Download
Get `ExileForgePlugin-PoE1.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest), extract the whole zip into a folder of your own and run `ExileForgePoe1.exe`. Needs Windows 10/11 x64 and the free **.NET 10 Desktop Runtime** (Microsoft); `INSTALL_GUIDE.txt` in the zip explains where to get it. Path of Exile 1 only.

Keep the folder somewhere you can write to (for example Documents or `C:\ExileForgePoe1`), not in `Program Files`: the app updates itself in place and needs to replace its own files. Do not run the program from inside the zip, and keep all the files together.

## Updates
When a new version is out, the app tells you. Press **Update** and it downloads the new version, checks it, replaces its own files in the same folder (your settings are kept) and starts again. If the automatic update fails, download the zip from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and extract it over your current folder.

After a Path of Exile 1 patch, the plugins may stop working until a new version of the app is out. Update as soon as the app offers it.

## Read this first
- Exile Forge Plugin **reads the memory of the game**, and some plugins (Auto Flask, Auto Loot, Auto Stash) **send clicks and key presses for you**.
- GGG's policy does not allow this and it can lead to **account bans**. Switch on only what you need, and use it at your own risk.
- Radar, Kill Tracker, Beast Helper and Unique Finder only read the game; the automation plugins are off until you switch them on.

## Plugins
- **Kill Tracker**: kills per map by rarity (Normal / Magic / Rare / Unique), per map and per session, with a map timer.
- **Radar**: the outline of the whole area, icons for monsters and objects, far league contents and walking paths on the large map (Tab) and the minimap; custom rules for monsters and objects.
- **Beast Helper**: marks the expensive Bestiary / Harvest beasts and any path you add.
- **Unique Finder**: marks the uniques you list when they lie on the ground, even unidentified ones.
- **Auto Loot**: picks up the drops around you while you are idle.
- **Auto Stash**: a Transfer button next to your gold stores the backpack into the stash tab you choose.
- **Auto Flask**: uses your life and mana flasks below the limits you set.
